How to Play

  • Roll the dice and race to reach **Tile 100**.
  • Ladders boost your position forward on the board.
  • Landing on special tiles requires answering **Philippine Trivia Questions**.
  • Answer correctly to maintain your spot; wrong answers send you back to your previous tile!
